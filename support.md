# Plane Radio Support

Plane Radio (on the App Store as **Plane Radio: AM Finder**) finds the AM broadcast stations you can tune on your aircraft's ADF: the strongest stations around you or any airport, the Class A "blowtorch" stations, and which AM stations carry pro and college games.

Questions or problems? Email **[planeradio@sothereiwas.us](mailto:planeradio@sothereiwas.us)**. Spotted a wrong station or frequency? Please tell me which screen and station; corrections are very welcome.

## Free and Premium

- **Free:** Blow Torch Stations (every Class A station, with its station page, map and Listen Live link), and the So There I Was podcast.
- **Plane Radio Premium**: Near Me, Nearest to Airport, and Sports. Choose **yearly** ($3.99 a year in the US, with a 14-day free trial for new subscribers) or **Lifetime** ($14.99 once: no subscription, nothing to renew or cancel).

## Canceling your free trial or subscription

Canceling is easy, and you won't be charged if you cancel at least one day before your trial ends.

**Canceling doesn't cut you off.** If you cancel during your free trial, you keep every Premium feature for the rest of the trial; it simply won't turn into a paid subscription. If you cancel a paid subscription, Premium keeps working until the end of the year you've paid for. So it's safe to cancel early, right after you start the trial, and still use the whole two weeks.

- **During your free trial:** tap **Cancel Free Trial** on Plane Radio's home screen.
- **Any time:** in Plane Radio, tap the **ⓘ** button, then **Manage Subscription** (or **Cancel Free Trial**).
- **Or on your iPhone or iPad:** open **Settings**, tap your name, then **Subscriptions**, then **Plane Radio**.


## Redeeming a code

Got a Plane Radio code? Open Plane Radio, tap **ⓘ**, then **Redeem a Code** (it's also on the Premium sign-up screen), and enter it. You can also redeem it in the App Store: tap your account picture, then **Redeem Gift Card or Code**.

## Switching from yearly to Lifetime

You can buy Lifetime at any time. Apple can't cancel a subscription for you, so Plane Radio asks you to: right after you buy Lifetime, tap **Cancel Yearly Plan** (it also stays on the home screen until you do). If you were still in your free trial, you won't be charged anything more. If you'd already paid for the year, you can ask Apple to refund that charge at **[reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com)**.

## Refunds

Apple handles all payments, so refunds are requested from Apple at **[reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com)**. Plane Radio never sees your payment details.

## Restoring Premium on a new device

Premium follows your Apple Account. On a new iPhone or iPad, sign in with the same Apple Account, open Plane Radio, tap **ⓘ**, then **Restore Purchases**.

## Common questions

**Does it work without a cell signal?** Yes. The station and airport lists are built into the app, and Near Me uses GPS, which works in airplane mode. Listen Live, maps and new podcast episodes need an internet connection.

**Near Me says it can't find my position.** Make sure location is allowed for Plane Radio in **Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services**. In an aircraft, a window seat helps the GPS.

**A Listen Live stream won't play a game.** Streams are run by each station, not by Plane Radio. Many stations block NFL, NBA and MLB games online, or limit streams to listeners in their area.

**Does Plane Radio tune my radio?** No. It tells you what to tune; it doesn't control your aircraft's radio and isn't a navigation or ATC tool. Always navigate with approved sources.

**When should I use it?** Only when it's safe and permitted, such as in cruise, and never during critical phases of flight.

## Privacy

Plane Radio collects no data about you: no accounts, no ads, no tracking. See the [privacy policy](https://github.com/SoThereIWas/planeradio-data/blob/main/privacy.md).
