# Plane Radio Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 23, 2026_

Plane Radio helps pilots find AM broadcast stations to tune on an aircraft's ADF. This policy explains what the app does with information. In short: **Plane Radio does not collect, store, sell, or share your personal information.**

## Location

If you use **Near Me**, the app asks permission to use your device's location. Your location is used **only on your device**, to work out which AM stations are strongest near you. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and it isn't stored after you leave that screen. You can turn location access off at any time in the Settings app; the rest of Plane Radio keeps working.

## What the app connects to

Plane Radio's station and airport lists are built into the app and work offline. When you're online, the app may:

- **Download an updated list of station stream links** from our public data file on GitHub. This is a plain file download; it doesn't identify you.
- **Load the So There I Was podcast feed** and episode audio from sothereiwas.us, when you open the podcast.
- **Show maps** using Apple Maps, under Apple's privacy policy.
- **Open a station's stream or website in Safari** when you tap Listen Live. Those streams are run by the stations, not by Plane Radio, and the stations' own privacy policies apply.

Like any internet request, these servers can see your device's IP address as part of delivering the content. Plane Radio doesn't add any identifier, and we don't receive or keep logs of who uses the app.

## Downloads and settings

Podcast episodes you download, and a few settings (such as your US / Canada choice), are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

## Purchases

Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details; Apple tells the app only whether your subscription is active. See Apple's privacy policy for how Apple handles purchases.

## No accounts, tracking, or ads

Plane Radio has no sign-in or accounts, no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

## Children

Plane Radio is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
